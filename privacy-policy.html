GeniRun — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Short version: GeniRun is a running game. We collect your location only while you run to record your route, award points, and mark game tiles on the map. We also collect your Google account name/email when you sign in. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion of your account and all data at any time by emailing us.

1. Who we are

GeniRun ("we", "us") operates the GeniRun mobile application and the GeniRun API (genirun.onrender.com). Contact: [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

We do not collect: contacts, photos, microphone/audio content, browsing history, or advertising identifiers for ads. Storage permission is used only for saving/sharing your run results. Audio permission is used only for local music playback while you run.

3. Background location

GeniRun accesses your location in the background only while a run is in progress. Background access exists so your run continues to be recorded when the screen turns off. Location tracking starts when you press "Start run" and stops immediately when you press "Finish". We never use background location for marketing, profiling, or when the app is closed and no run is active.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed only by these services, strictly to run GeniRun:

5. Storage & retention

Run and account data are stored on our backend database while your account exists. Tokens are stored on your device in secure storage. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data from the backend.

6. Your rights

You may at any time: request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and all associated data. To do so, email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email linked to your GeniRun account. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

GeniRun is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
